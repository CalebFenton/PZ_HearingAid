description=Took Hard of Hearing for the points and regret it? Find a hearing aid. Most of the ones lying around are broken: grab a screwdriver and some electronics scrap and fix it, or get lucky and find one that still works.
description=
description=[h2]Tiers[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid[/b]: most of the aids you find. Doesn't work. Repair it (Electrical 2) or dismantle it for parts.
description=[*][b]Hearing Aid[/b]: about 1 in 5 found aids. Hard of Hearing becomes normal hearing; Deaf becomes Hard of Hearing. About 2 in-game days per battery.
description=[*][b]Efficient Hearing Aid[/b]: about 1 in 150 found aids, or optimize a hearing aid (Electrical 4). Same effect, about 6 days per battery.
description=[*][b]Boosted Hearing Aid[/b]: craft only (Electrical 8). Gives Keen Hearing; Deaf becomes normal hearing. About 4 days per battery.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Right-click a hearing aid to add or remove a battery and to switch it on or off. It only works while worn, switched on and charged; switch it off whenever you don't need it. The tooltip shows the charge. Upgrades keep the battery; dismantling gives it back.
description=
description=People wore their hearing aids, so most turn up on corpses: about 1 in 65 ordinary zombies, 1 in 10 retirees, and 1 in 20 hospital patients, following 1994 US survey data. Others lie on nightstands and in bathrooms, dressers, side tables, hospital bedside tables, waiting rooms, and lost and found boxes. Doctors' desks hold new ones.
