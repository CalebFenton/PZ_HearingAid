description=[h2]The aids[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/tiers.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid[/b]: most of the aids you find. Does nothing until you repair it.
description=[*][b]Hearing Aid[/b]: Hard of Hearing becomes normal hearing, and Deaf becomes Hard of Hearing. About 2 days per battery.
description=[*][b]Efficient Hearing Aid[/b]: the same, for about 6 days per battery. Rarely found, so you'll usually optimize a hearing aid.
description=[*][b]Boosted Hearing Aid[/b]: Keen Hearing, even for a character whose hearing was normal, and Deaf becomes normal hearing. About 4 days per battery. Crafted only.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Finding one[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/corpse.png[/img]
description=Most aids are still in their owners' ears, and the owners won't mind if you take them. Retirees carry them most often, so search the dead in nursing homes, trailer parks, golf courses, country clubs and rich neighborhoods. Hospital patients come next. Off the ear, check hospital bedside wardrobes, doctors' desks, waiting rooms and lost and found boxes, and the nightstands, dressers and bathrooms of houses. Pharmacies didn't sell hearing aids in 1993, so they have none.

description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/recipes.png[/img]
description=Every recipe needs a table, light, a screwdriver, tweezers, and reading glasses or a magnifier. The new aid keeps the old one's battery.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Repair[/b] (Electrical 2): earbuds for a new speaker, an alcohol wipe for the corroded contacts, and glue.
description=[*][b]Optimize[/b] (Electrical 4): a digital watch for its low-power chip, electrical wire, and glue.
description=[*][b]Boost[/b] (Electrical 8): an amplifier, a microphone, two electrical wires, epoxy, and a scalpel, which you keep.
description=[/list]
description=A screwdriver alone takes any aid apart for scrap electronics and gives the battery back.
