description=
description=Took Hard of Hearing for the points and regret it? Find a hearing aid. Most of the ones you find are broken, so sit down at a table with a screwdriver, tweezers, your reading glasses and a pair of earbuds and fix it, or get lucky and find one that still works.
description=
description=[h2]The aids[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/tiers.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid[/b]: most of the aids you find. Doesn't work. Repair it (Electrical 2) or dismantle it for parts.
description=[*][b]Hearing Aid[/b]: Hard of Hearing becomes normal hearing; Deaf becomes Hard of Hearing. About 2 in-game days per battery.
description=[*][b]Efficient Hearing Aid[/b]: a lucky find, or optimize a hearing aid (Electrical 4). Same effect, about 6 days per battery.
description=[*][b]Boosted Hearing Aid[/b]: craft only (Electrical 8). Gives Keen Hearing; Deaf becomes normal hearing. About 4 days per battery.
description=[/list]

description=[*][b]Repair[/b] (Electrical 2): earbuds for a new speaker, an alcohol wipe for the contacts, and a dab of glue.
description=[*][b]Optimize[/b] (Electrical 4): a digital watch for its low-power chip, electrical wire, and glue.
description=[*][b]Boost[/b] (Electrical 8): an amplifier, a microphone, two electrical wires, epoxy, and a scalpel.
