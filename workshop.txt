title=Hearing Aid
description=Battery-powered hearing aids that let Deaf and Hard of Hearing survivors work their way back to better hearing.
description=
description=Took Hard of Hearing for the points and regret it? Find a hearing aid. Most of the ones lying around are broken: grab a screwdriver and some electronics scrap and fix it, or get lucky and find one that still works.
description=
description=[h2]Tiers[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid[/b]: common. Doesn't work. Repair it (Electrical 2) or dismantle it for parts.
description=[*][b]Hearing Aid[/b]: rare. Hard of Hearing becomes normal hearing; Deaf becomes Hard of Hearing. About 2 in-game days per battery.
description=[*][b]Efficient Hearing Aid[/b]: very rare, or optimize a hearing aid (Electrical 4). Same effect, about 6 days per battery.
description=[*][b]Boosted Hearing Aid[/b]: craft only (Electrical 8). Gives Keen Hearing; Deaf becomes normal hearing. About 4 days per battery.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Right-click a hearing aid to add or remove a battery and to switch it on or off. It only works while worn, switched on and charged; switch it off whenever you don't need it. The tooltip shows the charge. Upgrades keep the battery; dismantling gives it back.
description=
description=Found in bathroom cabinets, nightstands, side tables, junk drawers, doctors' offices, hospital wardrobes, pharmacies, optometrists, electronics stores, and on older zombies.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=Battery life per tier, chance a found hearing aid still has a battery, loot multipliers for broken and working aids, how deafness is handled, and whether boosted aids can be crafted.
