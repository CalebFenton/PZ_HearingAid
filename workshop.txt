description=Take Deaf or Hard of Hearing and work your way out of it. Most hearing aids you find are broken. Repair one, and a Hard of Hearing character hears normally. Upgrade it twice, and a Deaf character hears normally too, while anyone else gets Keen Hearing. Working hearing aids run on batteries.
description=
description=[h2]Hearing aid models[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/tiers.png[/img]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid[/b]: most of the hearing aids you find. Does nothing until you repair it.
description=[*][b]Hearing Aid[/b]: Hard of Hearing becomes normal hearing, and Deaf becomes Hard of Hearing. About 2 days per battery.
description=[*][b]Efficient Hearing Aid[/b]: the same, for about 6 days per battery. Rarely found, so you'll usually optimize a hearing aid.
description=[*][b]Boosted Hearing Aid[/b]: Keen Hearing, even for a character whose hearing was normal, and Deaf becomes normal hearing. About 4 days per battery. Crafted only.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Finding one[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/corpse.png[/img]
description=Most hearing aids in Knox County are still in their owners' ears, and the owners won't mind if you take them. Retirees carry them far more often than anyone else, so search the dead in nursing homes, trailer parks, golf courses, country clubs and rich neighborhoods. Hospital patients come next, in their gowns and bathrobes. Off the ear, check hospital bedside wardrobes, doctors' desks, waiting rooms and lost and found boxes, and the nightstands, dressers and bathrooms of houses. Pharmacies didn't sell hearing aids in 1993, so they have none.
description=
description=[h2]Crafting[/h2]
description=[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CalebFenton/PZ_HearingAid/master/art/promo/images/recipes.png[/img]
description=Every recipe needs a table, light, a screwdriver, tweezers, and reading glasses or a magnifier. The new hearing aid keeps the old one's battery.
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Repair[/b] (Electrical 2): earbuds for a new speaker, an alcohol wipe for the corroded contacts, and glue.
description=[*][b]Optimize[/b] (Electrical 4): a digital watch for its low-power chip, electrical wire, and glue.
description=[*][b]Boost[/b] (Electrical 8): an amplifier, a microphone, two electrical wires, epoxy, and a scalpel, which you keep.
description=[/list]
description=A screwdriver alone takes any hearing aid apart for scrap electronics and gives the battery back.
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Battery Life[/b]: in-game hours a full battery lasts, for each working model.
description=[*][b]Electrical Level[/b]: the level each recipe needs. 0 removes the requirement.
description=[*][b]Broken Hearing Aid Loot[/b] and [b]Working Hearing Aid Loot[/b]: multiply how often each spawns. 0 disables them.
description=[*][b]Found With Battery Chance[/b]: how often a working hearing aid you find still holds a battery.
description=[*][b]Handle Deafness[/b]: whether hearing aids help a Deaf character, and how much.
description=[*][b]Enable Boosted Hearing Aids[/b]: whether you can boost an Efficient Hearing Aid.
description=[/list]
